National Evangelism Day invites Christians everywhere to share Jesus with visiting children and parents on October 31. No costume, decorations, Halloween observance, religious speech at the doorstep, expensive candy, or complicated training required. No shame or pressure. Offer candy within budget along with a clearly written free Christian gospel handout and a kind smile. The original handout is titled **You Are Loved More Than You Know**, prints two per sheet, preferably single-sided with blank back for an optional handwritten church invitation, and includes a QR code to free children's digital picture book *Levi's Cookie Adventure* (a child takes a chocolate chip cookie after his mother tells him not to, opening a discussion of sin, forgiveness and Jesus). The separate free guide helps explain the gospel to people, especially children. **AdamOrtega.com** is the one clear call-to-action. The gospel explains sin, Jesus dying for sins and rising, and grace received through repentant faith in Christ. Never imply giving a tract guarantees conversion. Real resource artwork must be placed using an authorized approved image or added after via reliable editor. The short film cannot display full detailed copy of the resources, so emphasize them visually and briefly in speech.
