## Essential distinctions and factual accuracy

- **What:** October 31, National Evangelism Day is an invitation to use an existing stream of families arriving at Christian homes for candy as an opportunity to share the gospel of Jesus Christ. The intended participants are Christians; the visitors are children **with parents or accompanying adults**. This is not an observance of Halloween; no decoration or costume, occult celebration, or lengthy porch sermon is expected.
- **How:** Print or purchase a suitable truthful Christian gospel tract. A free printable tract titled **You Are Loved More Than You Know** is available through **AdamOrtega.com**. Print two per sheet, one side only if leaving the reverse blank for an optional handwritten church invitation; place one tract alongside affordable good individually wrapped candy, preferably in a small clear treat bag, and welcome visitors under the porch light. A user-provided or purchased gospel tract is also acceptable; do **not** imply people must use Adam's particular handout.
- **Gospel:** The tract explains who Jesus is, that He died for our sins and rose again, and that forgiveness is offered through repentance and faith in Him. A tract is a communication tool, not a guarantee someone becomes a believer.
- **Digital children's picture book:** The **link is on the printable tract from AdamOrtega.com**. The story **is digital and available online**, NOT physically inside treat bags and NOT presented as a printed book being handed out. Parents can access the link/QR on the tract from their phones and read the online flipbook to their children later. Levi's story involves a child who disobeys his mother by taking a chocolate chip cookie and learns about Jesus and forgiveness. Use the actual authorized online picture book imagery when possible; do not fake published covers or written pages.
- **Free conversation guide:** A separate **free guide on AdamOrtega.com** is for Christian participants who **want help knowing what to say if a parent or child chooses to talk with them about Jesus at the door**. It is not something handed automatically to every child, not an ordinary guide to talking with children, and not a requirement to initiate a doorstop conversation. Respect the parent and only continue a conversation when welcomed.
- **Call to action:** Join National Evangelism Day on **October 31**. All of the listed ministry resources are free at **AdamOrtega.com**. Provide one large final URL only; no competing URLs or guessed pages.
